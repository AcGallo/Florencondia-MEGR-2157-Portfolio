# Portfolio Overview

This portfolio documents my development as a mechanical engineering student through coursework, design projects, analysis, and hands-on engineering work. Rather than presenting only finished results, the portfolio is organized to show the process behind each project, including the analysis performed, the decisions made, and how the final work was communicated.

## Course Assignments

The MEGR 2157 assignments document my engineering work throughout the semester. Each assignment focuses on different areas of mechanical engineering, including stress analysis, CAD, finite element analysis, mechanical design, engineering drawings, and component selection.

## Portfolio Structure

Engineering work throughout this portfolio follows the **Analyze → Decide → Communicate** framework:

- **Analyze** — Understand the problem using calculations, research, models, testing, or other engineering methods.
- **Decide** — Select and justify an engineering solution based on the available information.
- **Communicate** — Present the engineering work clearly enough that another person can understand the process and results.
